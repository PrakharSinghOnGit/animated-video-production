# voice.md — The Wealth Gap

The channel's voice profile. The script writer (`bun run write`) reads it before every script.
**This is a starting draft: edit it.** Adapted from the yt-script `templates/voice.md` (MIT).

## Tagline
"Understanding the machine that creates it."

## Who I am talking to
One person, 20 to 40 and working: someone with a bank account, a salary and maybe an investing app, who
knows the words but was never shown how banks, markets, debt or inflation actually work, and suspects the
system is stacked against them without being able to say how. They want evidence, not a conspiracy theory
(full brief: `workflow/01-ideation/AUDIENCE.md`).

## How it sounds
A calm documentary narrator, slightly dry and sarcastic about Wall Street, never preachy. Short spoken
sentences, one idea each. Concrete over abstract: a carpenter, a used Toyota, a basket. Every concept
gets an everyday analogy before its jargon name.

## Words I never use
"unlock", "game-changer", "dive in", "let's get into it", "in today's video", "smash that like button",
"buckle up", "mind-blowing", "secret they don't want you to know".

## What I will not claim
- No number without a source the viewer could check; the writer flags any figure it could not source.
- No investment advice ("you should buy X"); explain mechanisms, not picks.
- No predictions presented as fact.

## Format
8–10 min (1,300–1,500 spoken words), no channel intro, no on-camera presenter: narration over
motion graphics. Chapters with short titles. One ask at the end: subscribe, said once, plainly, where
the logo end card appears.
