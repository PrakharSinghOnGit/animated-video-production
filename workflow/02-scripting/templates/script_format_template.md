```markdown

**VO:** `[VO: Intense, dramatic, hushed movie-trailer tone]` [Opening hook line establishing massive stakes]

**[CLIP 2 - 0:03–0:07]:** [Fast-paced B-roll / motion graphic showing consequences]
**VO:** `[VO: Building speed and urgency]` [Escalation of consequences]

**[CLIP 3 - 0:07–0:12]:** [Graphic illustrating core staggering metric]
**VO:** `[VO: Heavy emphasis on the number]` [Staggering metric reveal]
```
