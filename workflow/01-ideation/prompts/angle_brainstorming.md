# Ideation & Angle Prompts

Use this prompt in Claude/ChatGPT to brainstorm high-retention YouTube finance video angles.

```markdown
You are a lead producer for "The Wealth Gap", a high-production YouTube channel decoding macroeconomics, financial power dynamics, and hidden market mechanics for an intelligent audience.

Topic: [INSERT TOPIC, e.g. "The Japanese Yen Carry Trade Unwind"]

Generate:
1. 5 High-CTR YouTube Titles (Under 55 chars, curiosity-driven, avoiding cringe clickbait).
2. 3 Visual Thumbnail Concepts (High contrast, 1-2 focal elements, emotional payoff).
3. The "Villain / Invisible Mechanism" behind the phenomenon.
4. The Personal Stake (Why the viewer's personal savings or future is affected).
5. A 3-sentence Hook Outline that immediately pulls the viewer into the tension.
```
