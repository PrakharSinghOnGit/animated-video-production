# Drafting: the critic's rubric

What each scored dimension means in this style; § = section of `STYLE.md`. Score each 1–5:
5 = a viewer would admire it, 4 = good, nothing a viewer notices, 3 = noticeable weakness, 2 = a viewer is bothered, 1 = broken.

| Dimension | What 5 looks like | What pulls it down (name the tiles) |
|---|---|---|
| **staging** (§1, §10) | One idea big in the middle (geometry 400–1500 px wide), small tracked labels and dimension lines around it, the border clear; Penny guides in roughly half the acts and never becomes the subject | Boxes of bullet text, a slide layout, several small ideas competing, content resting near the border, Penny larger than ~25 % of the height |
| **motion** (§5, §8) | Every shot is made out of the previous one (bend, curl, tile, extrude, unfold); springs on arrivals; something changes at least every 1.5 s | A new picture appearing from nothing (pop findings), crossfades, holds over 1.5 s that are not a figure being read, rubbery bounce on everything |
| **camera** (§6, §7) | Locked: front-on and still; depth only from objects turning; no cuts inside the act | Any pan, zoom, roll or shake (a short thump on the biggest hit is fine); a hard cut inside the act; camera-jump or cut findings |
| **sync** (§5 "Land on the word") | Every move that matters lands on its spoken word (the words under each grid tile show what is said when) | Late or early reveals, a named thing never drawn, a picture unrelated to the line |
| **legibility** (§4) | Geist 900 for big words and numbers (≥ 90 px on screen), labels 24–28 px tracked with a paper-colour outline; corner labels on every frame | Clipped or overlapping text, labels too small, heavy (800+) labels that clog, low contrast |
| **colour** (§2, §3) | One ink per chapter (paper, blueprint or hologram), six colours only, the accent rare: the one thing that matters | Glows, blur halos, gradients, particles, a fourth colour on Penny, more than one ink change in a chapter |
| **data** (§12, plan `data`) | Only the plan's sourced numbers; bars from zero; honest scales | A number not in the plan, a stretched scale, an unsourced figure drawn |

Caps: any confirmed **high** finding or any dimension at 1 caps the score at 5; any dimension at 2 caps it at 6.
