# Briefing: the critic's rubric

What each scored dimension means in this style; § = section of `STYLE.md`. Score each 1–5:
5 = a viewer would admire it, 4 = good, nothing a viewer notices, 3 = noticeable weakness, 2 = a viewer is bothered, 1 = broken.

| Dimension | What 5 looks like | What pulls it down (name the tiles) |
|---|---|---|
| **staging** (§1, §6 Framing, §8) | Every beat has a set: giant cut type, a sheet of shadow paper, a horizon. The beat's subject fills ≥ 1/3 of the frame height at its hit; figures act against huge type | Small objects on an empty flat ground. A diagram (same-sized labelled icons joined by arrows). A box with text or a chart in it. A wide shot that shrinks everything into thumbnails |
| **motion** (§5) | Arrivals spring in and land with a thump; secondary motion after hits; held things idle; rows stagger in; calm tempo | Things appear or vanish with no animation (pop findings). A held picture where nothing moves (frozen). Everything in at once. Sprinting walks, jittery speed |
| **camera** (§6) | One continuous take that arrives before each action, holds while it is read, drifts while holding; whips are events (≤ 1 per 20 s) | Jumps or hard cuts inside the act. The camera leaves before an action is finished. Whips as a habit. Text read only partially while the camera passes |
| **sync** (§5 first bullet) | Every hit is complete on its spoken word (the words under each grid tile show what is said when) | Reveals late (after the word) or early (stealing the reveal). A named thing never shown. A picture unrelated to the line |
| **legibility** (§4) | Impact text is big cut type, readable at thumbnail size, held ≥ chars ÷ 15 + 1.5 s; labels ≥ 30 px on screen; nothing clipped or overlapping | Clipped or overlapping text, label-sized type where the style wants type as architecture, text that flashes by, low contrast |
| **colour** (§2, §3) | Four flat inks per location; the money warm on a black keyline; ink on light or paper on dark; the ground turns (paper → hot → ink) at the act's turn | Gradients, glows, a fifth colour, colour on the same colour, a warm object on a warm ground, black props that read as letters, one ground for the whole act with no turn |
| **data** (§9, plan `data`) | Every number on screen is one of the plan's figures, with units | A number not in the plan, a wrong unit, a stretched scale |

Caps: any confirmed **high** finding or any dimension at 1 caps the score at 5; any dimension at 2 caps it at 6.
