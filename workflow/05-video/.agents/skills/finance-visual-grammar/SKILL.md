# Finance Visual Grammar Skill

## Visual Metaphors
- **Key Single Number:** `BigNumber` (count-up lands on spoken anchor word).
- **Multiple / Comparison:** `BarCompare` or `SizeCompare` (area proportional to value).
- **Time Trend:** `LineDraw` (annotated peak or inflection point).
- **Wealth Distribution / Inequality:** `IconGrid` (100 icons, 1 highlighted in gold).
- **Flow of Capital / Interbank Transfers:** `MoneyFlow` (nodes and particles).
- **Core Concept / Definition:** `TermCard` + rough-notation underline.
- **Data Credibility:** `SourceTag` on every data scene.

## Integrity Rules
- Never invent numbers or fake trendlines.
- Distinguish flows ($/day or $/year) from stocks ($ accumulated).
- Never render financial advice or speculative predictions.
