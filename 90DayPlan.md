Scripting Fixes — For Explainer ContentYour retention data shows the first 53 seconds lose as many viewers as the next 8 minutes combined. Fixes:
Open-loop ladder — plant three questions, answer one early (at ~20%), hold the biggest until ~80%.
Never explain a mechanism before establishing why the viewer should care. Consequence first, mechanism second.
Re-hook every 90 seconds at section boundaries — one line: "And here's where it gets worse."
Trace one named person through the whole video.
Restate the stake at the halfway mark — one sentence bridging to the second half.
Pattern interrupt every 45–60 seconds — a number on screen, a chart cut, the character returning.
The 60-second "so what" test — if a minute doesn't give the viewer something they gain, cut it or move it.
Caution: never hide a simple answer to manufacture tension. That reads as padding and kills the second half.7. Cadence Decision (reference)
